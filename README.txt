THE WHITE HOUSE RESTAURANT — WEBSITE CONCEPT
================================================
This is an independent website redesign concept for pitching purposes only.
It is NOT the restaurant's official website and has not been approved by the business.

Files:
- index.html (Home)
- about.html
- services.html
- menu.html
- gallery.html
- reviews.html
- contact.html
- book-now.html
- checkout.html
- faq.html
- privacy.html
- assets/style.css
- assets/site.js

Important:
- Demo banner is intentionally visible on every page.
- Menu items/prices are illustrative; verify with restaurant before use.
- Review cards are placeholders, not genuine reviews.
- Images are stock photos and must be replaced with owner-approved images before official use.
- Booking/contact forms do not submit data.
- Checkout does not take payment or place orders.
- No backend, database, reservation availability, order handling, email, or payment provider is connected.
- The restaurant appears in public listings with an existing website: https://whitehouserestaurant.net
- Contact the restaurant and get permission before representing any version as official.
- Open index.html locally to preview. For deployment, upload all files preserving folder structure.
